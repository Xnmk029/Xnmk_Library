Ward Handbook

One button, one beat

Press SPACE (any key, click or tap works) the instant the orbiting planet lands on the next tile.

The fire and ice planets swap roles on every landing: whoever lands becomes the new anchor.

The path is the rhythm

A straight tile is one beat. A tight turn is half a beat. A wide turn is a beat and a half. Loops are long notes.

You never have to measure angles — read the shape, then listen. Each tile plays a note of the melody.

Twirls ↺

A twirl tile flips the orbit direction. Straight lines stay straight, but a run of eighth notes becomes a zig-zag instead of a spiral.

Cardiac holds ♥ 7

Land on a heart tile and the orbit stretches over seven beats. Count the six pulses — 1 2 3 4 5 6 — and press on seven.

Pure Rhythm Doctor: do not watch, count.

Vitals

Misses drain the patient. Perfect landings bring them back. Flatline and you resume from the last checkpoint.

Prefer the old ways? Turn on Strict Mode in Settings: one mistake, back to the checkpoint.

Blackouts

Some passages kill the lights. The tiles fade, the music does not. Keep counting.

Feeling early or late all round? Run Calibrate Audio from the title screen.
