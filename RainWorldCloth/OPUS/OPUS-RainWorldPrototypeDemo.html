Try it: hold a direction into the staircase to watch the seam guard keep the hip from snagging on tile edges. Drop Posture stiffness to 120 and the spine sags onto its bend limit — Stand height stops doing anything at all. Push Substeps to 8 and the walk speed barely shifts, because friction is normalised against a reference rate rather than applied per substep.
