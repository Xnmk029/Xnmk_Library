Cedar Ridge Motorsport Park

Front-mid 6.2 L cross-plane V8, equal-length headers, true dual exhaust. Every sound you hear is synthesised from the crank angle in an AudioWorklet - no samples.

WS throttle / brake
AD steer
QE gear down / up
Space handbrake
Shift clutch
Z reverse
C camera (chase / cockpit / hood / cinematic)
T auto driver
R reset to the circuit
M mute
P pause
L sound / auto-driver laboratory

Headphones recommended: the two banks of the V8 leave through two separate tailpipes, so the cross-plane rumble is a stereo effect.
