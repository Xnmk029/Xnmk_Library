## 💡 How to Use

1. **Pick a domain**: Find your target evaluation domain in the tables above, then click the **📋 Copy** link for the desired project.
2. **Copy the prompt**: The link opens a GitHub Raw plain-text page. Use `Ctrl+A` → `Ctrl+C` to copy the entire prompt.
3. **Submit to LLM**: Paste the prompt into your target AI model's chat. **Do not modify technical constraints.**
4. **Evaluate results**: Click the **📋 Spec** link to view the acceptance criteria and scoring rubric in the project's README.
