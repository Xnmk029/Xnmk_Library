远征指南：如何重燃烈日

1. 符文锻造与合成 (Forging)

Slide every stone at once; equal stones fuse into the next tier and hurl that value at the Warden as damage.

↑ ↓ ← →W A S Dswipedrag

2. 热量与能量充能 (Heat & Charge)

Every fuse stokes Heat (up to ×1.60 damage). A move with no fuse lets it cool. Multi-fuse moves chain a combo multiplier on top.

At maximum heat your greatest stone becomes ⚡ charged — fusing it strikes for ×1.7.

3. 专注技能与碎片摧毁 (Focus & Shatter)

Press Space, then click any stone to obliterate it — a lifeline when the grid chokes. Focus refills as you descend.

4. 守卫者首领 (The Wardens)

Each Warden telegraphs its intents. The counter shows how many moves until it acts: it strikes, petrifies cells into slag, freezes stones, sows blight, floods junk, or seals a direction.

5. 空间崩塌机制 (Collapse)

Fill the grid with no legal move and the board collapses: heavy damage, and the three smallest tiers shatter. Not instant death — but it will kill you.

6. 古老遗物与契约 (Relics & Pacts)

Every cleared floor grants a boon. Pacts trade a curse for legendary power. Build synergy — relics flash when they fire.

7. 黑曜石尖塔 (The Spire)

18 floors, 3 acts. Elites at 4 · 10 · 16, Wardens-of-Acts at 6 · 12 · 18. Reach the Heart of the Forge and relight the sun — then descend forever.
