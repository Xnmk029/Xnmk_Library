How to play

Push every crate onto a marker stone. The whale can only push — it cannot pull, and it cannot shift two crates at once.

A crate shoved into a corner is stuck for good, so think before the last step of a run. Undo costs you nothing.

Par is the fewest pushes the level can be solved in — it is always reachable.
